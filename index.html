Our Story

A Taste of
North Africa

Gusto Bar à Couscous was born from Chef Aghiles' passion for the rich, aromatic traditions of North African cuisine. Every grain of couscous, every slow-simmered tajine is a tribute to generations of culinary heritage.

Located in the heart of Berkeley, we bring the warmth of Algerian hospitality to your table — with hand-crafted spice blends, fresh local produce, and recipes passed down through family.
